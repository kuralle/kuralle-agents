# LinkedIn style

- Put the sharpest claim, number, or tension in the first line.
- Develop one idea per post with short lines and generous white space.
- Include a concrete result, decision, or before-and-after detail.
- Deliver the useful point in the post instead of withholding it behind a link.
- Use at most one emoji and no more than five specific hashtags.
- End with one genuine question or one clear takeaway.

Read `references/format.md` when checking length and structure. Run `lint_against_style` before proposing the draft.
