# Harborlight Hotel operating handbook

## accessibility

ADA-accessible rooms are available on every floor, and suites offer roll-in showers. Accessibility requirements must be recorded during booking so an appropriate room can be assigned.

## business_center

The business center is open daily from 07:00 to 21:00. Meeting rooms seat eight and cost $40 per hour for up to eight hours. Secretarial help costs $35 per hour for up to four hours. Same-day printing and binding costs $25 flat.

## cancellation

Confirmed rooms can be cancelled free until 48 hours before check-in, with the refund returned to the payment method on file in two to five business days. Inside 48 hours, one room-night is retained and the remainder is refunded. The full stay is charged at booking. Taxes are 12 percent and extras are itemized.

## florist

The in-house florist offers a seasonal bouquet for $65, twelve roses for $95, and a centerpiece for $140. Same-day delivery is available before 14:00; later orders deliver the next morning. Delivery hours are 10:00–18:00. Read the gift-card message back before ordering.

## functions

Public events may be disclosed: live jazz in the lobby bar from 20:00 to 23:00 tonight, and the Coastal Light photography exhibition in the mezzanine gallery daily from 10:00 to 18:00. Private functions are confidential. Never confirm or deny a named private event or disclose its location; offer an events-office follow-up instead.

## group_bookings

Groups of 15 or more use a group inquiry, not individual booking. The provisional rate is 10 percent below standard, with one complimentary tour-leader room per 15 paid guests. Collect sponsor, contact, callback number, party size, room-share style, check-in, and nights. The group desk responds within two business days after credit review; never promise confirmation during chat.

## guest_privacy

Never confirm or deny whether a named person is staying, disclose a room number, or connect an outside caller to a room. There are no authority, friend, family, or surprise exceptions. Offer to log a message that will be passed along if possible. A message reference and the general 30-minute delivery target reveal nothing about guest presence.

## guest_services

Wake-up calls can be scheduled for a room at a concrete date and time. A second call follows after five minutes, then staff perform an in-person check. Laundry left before 09:00 returns the same day. Lost property is held for 90 days. Lobby workstations are available around the clock.

## guest_walks

For an overbooked confirmed stay, apologize and state that the hotel oversold the night. First re-accommodate the guest in an equal or better room at no added cost. If none fits, arrange the Harbor House two blocks away, including both room and taxi, and guarantee return on the supplied date. Record a manager callback if the guest remains dissatisfied.

## local_area

Downtown is about ten minutes by car or twenty-five minutes on foot. Muni is two blocks away and BART is a ten-minute walk. A pharmacy is within two blocks, non-emergency urgent care is five blocks south, and the nearest hospital is six blocks east. Do not invent exact addresses, telephone numbers, schedules, or opening hours not listed here.

## location_and_transport

The hotel is at 100 Kuralle Way, San Francisco. SFO is about thirty minutes by car. There is no shuttle. The hotel car costs $85, carries up to four with luggage, and must be booked for a concrete pickup time. Taxis usually cost $55–$70 and are hailed at the main entrance.

## payments_and_currency

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and US cash are accepted. A card is required for incidentals. Never collect full card numbers, security codes, passport numbers, social-security numbers, or bank credentials in chat. Major foreign currency can be exchanged by resident guests in person with a passport at the posted desk rate; never estimate that rate remotely.

## pets

Pets are welcome only in designated pet-friendly rooms for a $50 fee per stay. Service animals are welcome in every room at no charge. Guests travelling with a pet must say so during availability search and booking so the room assignment is valid.

## restaurant_dietary

Vegetarian and most dietary requirements are supported. Severe allergies must be recorded on the reservation and confirmed with the kitchen; never guarantee an allergen-free environment.

## restaurant_dining

Dinner seatings run 17:30–21:00. Dress is smart casual and no jacket is required. The dining room, terrace, and bar welcome children. Tables require weekend reservations; bar walk-ins are accepted. A private room seats twelve with advance booking.

## restaurant_menu

Dinner includes starters, salads, salmon, chicken, steak, pasta, burgers, vegetarian risotto, sides, desserts, and a full bar. Prices rotate. If a requested dish or price is not known, record a kitchen follow-up rather than inventing it.

## room_service

Room service uses the restaurant menu from 17:30 to 21:30. Takeout and third-party delivery are not offered.

## rooms_and_amenities

Rooms include a 55-inch television, mini-fridge, safe, iron, hair dryer, coffee machine, and blackout curtains. Suites have a separate sitting area. Cribs, rollaway beds, and connecting rooms are free requests subject to availability.

## safe_deposit

Every room has a guest-set electronic safe. Complimentary larger safe-deposit boxes are behind the front desk and require the registered guest to appear with photo ID. They are never opened or cleared remotely. The desk is staffed 24 hours.

## spa

The third-floor spa is open 08:00–20:00, with the last appointment at 19:00. Deep-tissue massage is $140 for 60 minutes; a facial is $120 for 50 minutes; personal training is $80 for 45 minutes; group yoga is $40 per person for 60 minutes.

## tours

Half-day city highlights leave the lobby at 09:00 and cost $65 per person. The full-day city and bay tour leaves at 08:30 and costs $110 per person. A private half-day car and guide costs $290 for up to four, normally leaving at 10:00.
