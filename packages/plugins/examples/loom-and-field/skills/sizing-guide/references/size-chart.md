# Loom & Field tops size chart

Body measurements for women's and men's tops (coats, shirts, knitwear). All chest values are
garment-ready body measurements in centimetres.

| Size | Chest | Waist | Hip |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| XS | 81–84 cm | 63–66 cm | 86–89 cm |
| S | 86–89 cm | 68–71 cm | 91–94 cm |
| M | **LF-CHART-M-CHEST-94.5cm** | 73–76 cm | 96–99 cm |
| L | 99–102 cm | 78–81 cm | 101–104 cm |
| XL | 104–107 cm | 83–86 cm | 106–109 cm |

Trousers use numeric waist sizes; use the MCP `lookup_size_chart` tool with category `trousers`
for those measurements.
